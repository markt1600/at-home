# Main bedroom bedside tables

The supplied walkthrough at 197–203 seconds and the owner's front-on bedroom
photograph show matching low ivory cabinets on both sides of the west headboard.
Each has two handle-free drawers, a recessed base and a black-stem lamp with a
white disc shade. The open-shelving side has a white dome diffuser and small
bedside electronics; the glass-block side has an oak tissue box and black speaker.

The authored units are approximately 520 mm wide, 530 mm deep and 485 mm high.
Their tops sit below the mattress; their backs meet the upholstered headboard,
with enough clearance for the adjoining shelving. Dimensions are fitted visual
estimates from the references, not measured manufacturer specifications.

Both furniture footprints are exported to the memory editor and used by player,
pet and hand-interaction collision checks. The Mops viewpoint at the foot of the
bed remains clear. The furniture, lamps and accessories use static material
batching with one additional material and no extra lights, textures or animation.
Private reference photographs and walkthrough frames remain outside the repository.
